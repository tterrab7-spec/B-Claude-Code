Contact sheet · prepared 9 Nov 2026 · for the 591 City Center Blvd approach

Who has sat at the table with CHKD on real estate

Five brokerages and one developer show up in the public record on Children's Hospital of The King's Daughters transactions since 2015. Two of them have represented CHKD directly. The rest sat across the table or lease to CHKD as landlord agents, which still means they know the account and the people who sign. Contacts below are from firm press releases, listing brochures and broker directories; direct lines marked "verify" come from third-party directories.

Deals reviewed: 2020 Norfolk Southern tower purchase, CHKD Thrift leases 2015–2026, former CHKD Imaging Center listing, Tech Center and Landstown buildings, Harbour View and Butler Farm health centers

How to use this

Lead with Divaris. Gerald Divaris, Michael Divaris and Jason Oliver represented the TowneBank and CHKD joint venture when it bought the Norfolk Southern headquarters tower in June 2020, and Divaris still manages and leases that building for the venture. That is a buyer-side relationship with CHKD's finance and legal leadership, the same people who control 591 City Center Blvd. Tell Divaris you have a credit-tenant utility headquarters for CHKD's 14.93 vacant acres and let them carry it to Kathy Abshire. A broker with a fee on the other side of the table is a motivated intermediary, and it gets you a warm introduction instead of a cold call.

Second call is Chris Zarpas at S.L. Nusbaum, who is marketing the former CHKD Imaging Center at 6170 Cleveland St in Norfolk right now. Whether CHKD or a landlord owns that building, he is the broker currently touching CHKD-vacated real estate, and Nusbaum has represented CHKD Thrift as tenant.
