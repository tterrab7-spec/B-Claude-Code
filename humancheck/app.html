    Hidden marks

    Scans for zero-width characters, invisible joiners, unusual spaces, direction controls, variation selectors and look-alike letters from other alphabets. These survive copy and paste and are the easiest way a document gets flagged or traced.

    Style fingerprints

    Curly quotes, em dashes, ellipsis characters and other typography that word processors and AI tools insert but people rarely type.

    Writing signals

    Rhythm, vocabulary and structure patterns that detectors key on. Each one shows how much it moved the score.
