Reaching Costco about the Newport News remainder

Renee Rutherford is the right starting point, but she is not the only door. This brief gives her title, base and public record, the Costco corporate and regional chain above her, Costco's exclusive outside broker for the Southeast, and the local players who actually carried the Newport News approvals. Direct lines marked "verify" come from third-party directories.

Costco closed on 32.37 acres at 12470, 12490 and 12500 Jefferson Ave on 31 Jul 2025 for $28MStore opens November 2026; about 12 acres in two parcels to be sold off

What the record says about the 12 acres

The conditional use permit (CU-2025-0003, Planning Commission 9-0 on 21 May 2025, City Council 10 Jun 2025) covers a 20.23-acre parcel. The staff report says Costco would buy all 32.37 acres from Ferguson, record a boundary line adjustment splitting the land into three parcels, build on the largest, and that "two remaining parcels (about 12 acres) will be held for future sale." News coverage put it plainly: "the remaining land will be split into two parcels, which the wholesale giant eventually will sell off." The Newport News EDA waived its repurchase rights on the Ferguson land and signed a $3.25M participation agreement with Costco for road and transit improvements at Jefferson and Bland. So the 12 acres are Costco's to sell, the city already expects a sale, and the EDA has a direct line into Costco from the agreement.
