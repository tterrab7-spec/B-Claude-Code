Methodology

How the math works

The engine is open about every formula. These definitions match the code that produces your report.

Core metrics

MetricFormula
Net operating incomeGross rent − vacancy + other income − taxes, insurance, HOA, utilities, maintenance, CapEx reserve, management
Cap rateYear-1 NOI ÷ purchase price
Cash-on-cash(NOI − annual debt service) ÷ (down payment + closing + rehab)
DSCRNOI ÷ annual debt service
Debt yield (commercial)Year-1 NOI ÷ loan amount
GRMPrice ÷ gross annual rent
Break-even occupancy(Operating expenses + debt service) ÷ gross potential income
IRRRate where NPV of [−cash invested, annual cash flows, sale proceeds net of selling costs and loan payoff] = 0, solved by Newton-Raphson with bisection fallback
Equity multipleTotal cash returned ÷ cash invested
Flip MAO(ARV × (1 − selling %) ÷ (1 + target ROI) − renovation − holding) ÷ (1 + buyer closing %)
Commercial exitForward (year N+1) NOI ÷ exit cap, less selling costs
Loan sizingmin(LTV × price, NOI ÷ (min DSCR × annual loan constant))
Static land residual(Lot revenue × (1 − margin) − hard, fees, soft, contingency) ÷ (1 + closing + carry)

Investment score weights

Residential

  • Cap rate 20 (3% → 8%)
  • Cash-on-cash 20 (0% → 10%)
  • DSCR 15 (1.0x → 1.5x)
  • Cash flow / unit 15 ($0 → $300)
  • GRM 15 (18 → 9)
  • IRR 15 (5% → 16%)

Commercial

  • Cap vs. market 20 (−100 → +100 bps)
  • DSCR 15 (1.0x → 1.5x)
  • Cash-on-cash 15 (2% → 10%)
  • Debt yield 15 (7% → 11%)
  • Levered IRR 20 (6% → 16%)
  • WALT or occupancy 15

Land

  • Land % of finished lot 35 (35% → 15%)
  • Margin at ask 35 (5% → 25%)
  • Project IRR 30 (8% → 25%)

Points scale linearly between the two thresholds shown. 75+ is Strong Buy, 60-74 Buy, 45-59 Negotiate, under 45 Pass.

Lead scores

Deal Finder, Listing Prospector and Land Finder scores are sums of named signals capped at 100. Each signal and its points appear on the record. Examples: pre-foreclosure +24, delinquent taxes +20, expired listing +24 (agent), 7-13 year tenure +18 (agent), loan maturity within 24 months +16 (agent, commercial), 20-300 acres +22 (land).
