Residential 1-4 units

Rent from RentCast AVM or HUD FMR, taxes from the locality's actual rate, insurance and vacancy from market data. Cap rate, CoC, DSCR, GRM, break-even occupancy, 1% rule, IRR, equity multiple and after-tax IRR with recapture.

Bidding & negotiation

Seven offer prices from -15% to +15%, a solved cash-flow break-even price, and the exact price needed to reach Buy or Strong Buy. A rate-by-price heatmap shows how much a buydown matters.

Fix & flip MAO

Cosmetic, midrange and major renovation tiers with cost per square foot, hold time, value recoup and selling costs, solved for the maximum allowable offer at your target ROI.

Strategy fit

Buy-and-hold, flip, house hack and short-term rental scored side by side, with the monthly cash flow each strategy would produce.

Commercial rent rolls

Line-item tenants with NNN/MG/gross recoveries, escalations, lease expirations, renewal probability, downtime, TI and leasing commissions. Loan sized at the lesser of LTV and DSCR; levered and unlevered IRR on an exit at forward NOI.

Land residual

Lot yield from acreage, net developable share and density, then finished lot value, site work, proffers and impact fees, soft costs, entitlement time and takedown pace. Static and DCF residual land value.
