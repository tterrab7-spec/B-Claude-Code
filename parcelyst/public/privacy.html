Privacy Policy

Template policy. Have counsel review before launch.

What we collect

Account details (name, email, role), billing records from Stripe (we never see full card numbers), the analyses, searches and lists you create, and basic usage logs.

How we use it

To run the service, enforce plan limits, send account email and improve the product. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

With processors that run the service (hosting, Stripe, data providers you query) and, only if you request a tax review, with the CPA partner you choose to contact.

Retention and deletion

Delete analyses and imports at any time. Close your account and we delete your data within 30 days, except billing records we must keep.
