About

Built by developers who got tired of guessing.

Parcelyst comes out of real development and brokerage work in Virginia: hundreds of finished lots delivered to national builders, affordable multifamily, and industrial and mixed-use advisory. We needed one tool that could underwrite a rental, a rent roll and a raw parcel with the same rigor, then tell us who to call next.

Show the work

Every report lists each input, where it came from and when. Lenders, partners and clients can check any number without calling us.

Local before national

A national average tax rate is wrong almost everywhere. We load each locality's real rate, rent baseline and development costs before a market goes live.

Rank, don't dump

A list of 40,000 absentee owners is not a strategy. Every lead carries a score and the specific reasons behind it.

What we will not do

  • Claim "zero assumptions." Underwriting is assumptions; ours are visible and editable.
  • Show logos of firms that do not use us.
  • Sell your saved analyses, searches or imported lists. Your imports are private to your account.
  • Present demo data as real. Synthetic demo records are labeled on every screen.
