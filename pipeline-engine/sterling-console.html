The signal of record: when the notice lands

Virginia assesses locally and every locality runs its own calendar. The notice date is the trigger. A property manager who gets a 12 percent increase in the mail has 30 to 60 days to act, and almost none of them have an appraiser lined up. Sterling's pitch window is the 60 days before the notice, which for most of Virginia is right now through January.

What happens when you approve

1. EnrichClaude reveals the work email and direct dial on Apollo. One credit each, charged only for approved leads.
2. DraftThe play's first touch is personalized to the lead: their locality's notice date, their portfolio type, the one question.
3. You see itDrafts come back to you in chat before anything sends. Edit or kill any of them.
4. Send and watchOnce a Sterling sending mailbox is linked in Apollo, approved leads enroll in the sequence. Replies come back here within the hour.

Rejections matter as much as approvals. Each one tells Claude which titles, firms and property types to stop surfacing. Ten rejections in a row on residential property managers and that filter tightens on the next pull.
