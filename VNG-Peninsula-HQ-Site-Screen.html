Virginia Natural Gas Peninsula Headquarters: 8-acre-plus land options

Twenty-seven parcels and tracts that clear the 8-acre floor, ranked by how well they fit a corporate headquarters for a regulated gas utility: by-right office or industrial zoning, public ownership or a motivated seller, interstate access, and a site the owner will actually sell you. Preferred target is 10 acres; everything here is 8 or more, or can be carved from a larger tract. Includes a dedicated pass on company-held land that is not on the market, led by the CHKD tract at City Center.

Read this before you pitch VNG

VNG is not publicly shopping for a new corporate headquarters. Corporate HQ stays at 544 S. Independence Blvd in Virginia Beach, and the company broke ground in May 2025 on a $50 million Operations Headquarters at 401 Clearfield Ave in Chesapeake for about 150 people, due late 2026 or early 2027. The company has 400-plus employees and lists Newport News as a regional office (a 2020 Virginia 811 record puts it at 746 Diligence Drive; unverified today). The credible Peninsula story is a consolidated Peninsula regional headquarters and service center, or a corporate relocation after the Chesapeake build settles. Size the pitch accordingly.

What 10 acres buys: roughly 120,000 to 160,000 SF of two- or three-story office with surface parking at 4 per 1,000 SF, or 80,000 to 100,000 SF of office plus a secured fleet and materials yard. A fleet yard is the usual reason a utility needs 12 to 15 acres instead of 10, so the 14-plus-acre options below are not oversize.

Scoring matrix

Five factors, each scored 0 to 3. Zoning scores by-right office or industrial use for a utility HQ. Control scores whether a public owner or active listing means you can actually transact. Image scores interstate visibility, address quality and surrounding amenities. Readiness scores utilities, remediation and site work. Acreage scores fit to a 10 to 15 acre program without buying a farm.

Company-held land: the CHKD tract and others not on the market

Land a company or institution bought for its own expansion and never built on. None of it is listed, so the play is a direct conversation with the owner. Expect a slower yes and a cleaner site than anything the EDA is marketing.

22 · CHKD land at Rock Landing Dr & City Center Blvd

Newport News
Children's Hospital of The King's Daughters · Oyster Point campus, 11783 Rock Landing Dr · one block east of Jefferson Ave
Acres
Roughly 12 to 15 unbuilt acres by your estimate. Public records I could reach do not state the parcel size; confirm with the city parcel viewer before the call.
Owner
CHKD (Children's Health System, Norfolk). The campus opened with a 43,000 SF Health & Surgery Center as phase 1 of a five-phase master plan; a hydrotherapy wing (2005) and an ambulatory surgery addition followed. Phases 4 and 5 were never built, which is why the land sits idle.
Zoning
C-4 Oyster Point Business District, the same designation as the City Center Expansion Area next door. Office HQ is a by-right use (verify on the parcel).
Why it fits
City Center address, I-64 at J. Clyde Morris two minutes away, Class A neighbors, utilities in the street. Pair it with the adjoining 8.19 acres at 895 City Center Blvd (site 23) and you are above 20 acres with one owner conversation and one broker call.

CHKD has no public plan to expand in Newport News and just committed its capital to Norfolk (new cardiovascular ICU, Olney Road construction through mid-2026). A hospital system holding non-core land through a capital cycle is a seller at the right number, and a long-term ground lease is a credible fallback that keeps the asset on their books.

Call first
Jason Mueller, Vice President, Facilities and Support Services. His portfolio explicitly includes Real Estate, Construction and Facilities for every CHKD location (over 2 million SF). Architecture degree, Ohio State; MBA, UMGC.
Reach
CHKD main switchboard, 757-668-7000, ask for the Facilities and Support Services office. Profile: chkd.org, About Us, Leadership and Key Staff. No verified direct line or email found; CHKD addresses follow first.last@chkd.org, so the format is likely but unconfirmed.
Backups
Kathy Abshire, Senior Vice President of Finance and CFO (a land sale clears through her). Tara Estabrooks, VP Strategic Planning and Business Development (title per third-party directories; verify). Amy Sampson, President and CEO since Dec 2022, for a principal-to-principal approach.
Note
Older directories still list Tamika Harris in Mueller's role. She has moved on; do not open with her name.

Opening line that works: a regulated utility HQ is a credit tenant and a quiet neighbor to a pediatric surgery center. Lead with the ground-lease option, then let them ask about a sale.

Method and limits

Acreage, ownership and zoning come from city fact sheets, council and EDA actions, news coverage, VEDP site data and brokerage listing records. Several figures are dated: the Hampton Roads Center North price flyer is undated, the Oakland Industrial Park parcel sizes are from a 2019 VEDP list, and listing prices move. Parcel-level GIS was not available from this environment, so boundaries, exact acreage and current owner of record should be confirmed before any letter of intent. Map positions are schematic. Sites below 8 acres from the same public inventories (Warwick Village 7 ac, Sherwood 7.74 ac, Washington Ave 5.3 ac, Hampton Roads Center North 3.26 and 1.97 ac lots) were excluded.
