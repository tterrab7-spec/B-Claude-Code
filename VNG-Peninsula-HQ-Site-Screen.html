What VNG told us and what it means for the list

VNG wants to stay as close as possible to the City Center area of Newport News, near restaurants and shopping like City Center and Port Warwick, and near its existing office at 746 Diligence Dr in the Oyster Point submarket. That rules out industrial parks, waterfront brownfields and anything north of the airport. It leaves two sites that actually meet the brief, both at City Center, plus the Costco-owned remainder at Jefferson and Bland three miles up the road. Everything in Hampton is off the list.

Program sizing: 10 acres carries roughly 120,000 to 160,000 SF of two- or three-story office with surface parking at 4 per 1,000 SF, or 80,000 to 100,000 SF plus a secured fleet and materials yard. VNG's corporate HQ stays in Virginia Beach and its $50M Operations HQ in Chesapeake opens late 2026 or early 2027, so the Peninsula program is a consolidated regional headquarters, not the whole company.
