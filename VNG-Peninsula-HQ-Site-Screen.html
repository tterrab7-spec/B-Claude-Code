What changed, and what the two new sites really are

Costco is off: you spoke with them and the Jefferson Ave remainder is not an option. The City Center Expansion Area is off at your direction. CHKD's 591 City Center Blvd tract is still the only site that meets the brief VNG gave us (City Center address, walkable restaurants, a mile from 746 Diligence Dr, 10-plus acres, office by right), and we are still trying to reach CHKD. Program sizing: 10 acres carries roughly 120,000 to 160,000 SF of two- or three-story office with surface parking at 4 per 1,000 SF, or 80,000 to 100,000 SF plus a secured fleet and materials yard. VNG's corporate HQ stays in Virginia Beach and its $50M Operations HQ in Chesapeake opens late 2026 or early 2027, so the Peninsula program is a consolidated regional headquarters, not the whole company. Before either new site goes further, get VNG's Peninsula headcount and yard requirement in writing: Bland Blvd only makes sense with a yard, Enterprise Pkwy only without one.

2 · 615 Bland Blvd, former Continental / Vitesco plant

Newport News
Parcel 111000101 (APN 111.00-01-01) · southeast corner of Bland Blvd and Jefferson Ave, across Jefferson from the new Costco · I-64 Exit 255 about 1 mile · new to the list
Proximity
About 3 miles north of City Center and of 746 Diligence Dr up Jefferson Ave. Patrick Henry Mall and Jefferson Commons sit across the interchange; Costco's new warehouse and fuel station open directly across Jefferson Ave in November 2026. Retail-corridor and airport location, not the walk-to-lunch City Center address VNG described.
Acres
27.4 acres (city record, Crexi, buyer's release). Nearly three times the program. Most of it is under the building and its truck courts.
Building
Single-story manufacturing plant built 1971: 365,956 SF per city records, 375,556 SF on Crexi, 378,331 SF in the buyer's release. 13 dock doors, 22-foot clear. Siemens Automotive, then Siemens VDO, then Continental (which bought Siemens VDO in 2007) and its Vitesco Technologies powertrain unit made hydraulic engine components here with about 740 workers. Continental announced the closure in September 2019 for 2024; the WARN notices ran from December 2019.
Owner
Your parcel viewer and the city record show Bland Boulevard LLC, which bought the plant in 2007 for $22,000,000, timing and price consistent with a sale-leaseback from the manufacturer. The LLC's public filings use 11010 Jefferson Ave, the W.M. Jordan Company address, which points to a Jordan-affiliated investor group (unverified). On 29 September 2026 an affiliate of Phoenix Investors, Milwaukee, announced it had acquired the 378,331 SF property. Price undisclosed, no broker named. Assessor records lag deeds by weeks, so treat Phoenix as the owner and Bland Boulevard LLC as the seller until the city updates.
Zoning
M-1 Light Industrial. Offices are a customary M-1 use, but a stand-alone headquarters office on M-1 land needs a written determination from Newport News Planning before anyone relies on it. A utility operations center with fleet and materials yard is squarely within M-1.
Buyer's plan
Phoenix says it will reconfigure the layout and loading areas, update building systems and market the plant to "a broad range of manufacturing and light industrial users." LoopNet already shows 12,480 to 365,851 SF for lease. Phoenix is a value-add industrial landlord with 80 million SF; it buys closed plants to lease them, not to sell land.

Why it is on the list: you asked. What it is: an industrial reposition three miles from where VNG said it wants to be. The honest fit for VNG is not a headquarters pad but a consolidated operations base. The 27 acres take a fleet and materials yard easily, and a 60,000 to 100,000 SF office build-out inside or in front of the plant costs less than new construction. Two ways in: lease or buy a demised portion of the plant from Phoenix once its reconfiguration plan is set, or buy an outparcel on the Jefferson Ave or Bland Blvd frontage for a new office building, which Phoenix may entertain if it does not break truck circulation. A whole-site purchase is unlikely; Phoenix just closed and underwrote this as a lease-up.

HRC-1, Hampton Roads Center district. General office is what the district exists for; confirm the headquarters-office line in Hampton's Table of Uses (effective 1 Sept 2024) with Hampton Planning.
Fit
A move-in-ready 63,000 SF office, below the 80,000 to 160,000 SF program, with no expansion land. Works only if VNG's Peninsula headcount is smaller than the program assumed, or as an interim office while a City Center site is assembled.

Why it is on the list: you asked for all the information. What it is: the fastest and cheapest occupancy on the Peninsula (sign at $18 full service and be in by mid-2027, or buy a 63,000 SF Class A building that should trade in the $90 to $130 per SF range, roughly $5.5M to $8M) and the weakest match to what VNG told us. It is in Hampton, seven miles from City Center, on 5.46 acres with no yard. If VNG's real Peninsula program is 150 people and no fleet, this building deserves a tour; if it is the 10-acre headquarters with a yard, it does not.

What the record says about 615 Bland Blvd

Built 1971 as the Siemens Automotive plant on 27.4 acres at the corner of Bland Blvd and Jefferson Ave, parcel 111000101. Siemens VDO passed to Continental in 2007, the same year Bland Boulevard LLC bought the real estate for $22,000,000; that is a sale-leaseback by timing and price. Continental's Vitesco Technologies unit made hydraulic engine components here with about 740 workers until the closure Continental announced in September 2019 for 2024 (WARN notices from December 2019). The plant was marketed on Crexi and LoopNet as 375,556 SF on 27.40 acres, M-1, 13 docks, 22-foot clear, unpriced, with the listing noting the new Costco and fuel pad directly across the street. On 29 September 2026 an affiliate of Phoenix Investors, Milwaukee, announced the acquisition of the 378,331 SF property; the release names no seller, price or broker, says Phoenix will reconfigure the building and site for manufacturing and light industrial users, and lists Luke Herder for leasing. Newport News Daily covered the sale about six weeks ago and noted the city record still shows Bland Boulevard LLC as owner. Three sales appear in the city's history; the assessor's online estimate is irrelevant to a deal. Your parcel viewer matches the city record, which has not caught up to the deed.
