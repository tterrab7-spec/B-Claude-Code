What VNG told us and what it means for the list

VNG wants to stay as close as possible to the City Center area of Newport News, near restaurants and shopping like City Center and Port Warwick, and near its existing office at 746 Diligence Dr in the Oyster Point submarket. That rules out industrial parks, waterfront brownfields and anything north of the airport. It leaves two sites that actually meet the brief, both at City Center, and four others kept for leverage or as fallbacks. If City Center is non-negotiable, the Hampton and north Newport News entries below are bargaining chips, not destinations.

Program sizing: 10 acres carries roughly 120,000 to 160,000 SF of two- or three-story office with surface parking at 4 per 1,000 SF, or 80,000 to 100,000 SF plus a secured fleet and materials yard. VNG's corporate HQ stays in Virginia Beach and its $50M Operations HQ in Chesapeake opens late 2026 or early 2027, so the Peninsula program is a consolidated regional headquarters, not the whole company.

Tier 1: the two sites that meet the brief

1 · 591 City Center Blvd, vacant CHKD land

Newport News
Parcel 183-00-01-29 · Oyster Point submarket · recorded owner Childrens Real Estate LLC, a CHKD entity
Proximity
About 1 mile to VNG's current Newport News office at 746 Diligence Dr, under a mile to City Center at Oyster Point, about 1.5 miles to Port Warwick. Walk-to-lunch distance from the Marriott, Town Center and the City Center restaurants.
Acres
14.93 acres, vacant. Clears the 10-acre target with room for a fleet yard, or for a phase-two pad CHKD could keep.
Owner
Childrens Real Estate LLC, the title-holding entity for Children's Hospital of The King's Daughters. Purchased 11 Nov 2016 for $10,030,141 (about $672,000 per acre, $15.42 per SF). Held vacant for nearly ten years. The purchase predates the current CEO, who took over in December 2022.
Zoning
C-2 General Commercial. Office is a by-right use. The city's own stated proposed use is commercial, medical or office.
Why it fits
Same submarket as VNG's existing Newport News service center at 746 Diligence Dr, so a consolidation story writes itself. City Center address, I-64 at J. Clyde Morris, Jefferson Lab and Tech Center as neighbors, utilities in the street, no demolition, no remediation.

Ten million dollars of idle land is either a strategic hold under leadership review or dead capital. Either way the conversation is the same: CHKD's capital is going to Norfolk (new cardiovascular ICU, Olney Road construction through mid-2026), its Peninsula outpatient footprint is already served by the Rock Landing surgery center and the Tech Center urgent care, and a credit-tenant utility is the kind of buyer or ground tenant a nonprofit board can approve without a fight.

Related CHKD holding. The 7.95-acre Health & Surgery Center parcel at 11783 Rock Landing Dr (APN 196.00-01-60) is a separate CHKD property and stays in use. Site 23 (895 City Center Blvd) is a third party's medical campus, not part of this tract.

Call first
Kathy Abshire, Senior Vice President of Finance and Chief Financial Officer. Children's Real Estate, LLC sits in her reporting line, she carries the $10M basis on her balance sheet, and a monetization or ground lease is a finance decision before it is a facilities decision.
Second door
Kimberly Day, Senior Vice President and Chief Legal Officer, registered agent for Children's Real Estate, LLC at 601 Children's Lane. Any purchase agreement or ground lease is papered through her office. A one-page letter naming the parcel, the 2016 purchase and VNG as a credit tenant is appropriate here.
Reach
No published direct dials for either. Switchboard 757-668-7000, ask for the Office of the CFO or the Office of General Counsel by name. CHKD staff email follows First.Last@chkd.org (confirmed by a published supply chain address), so Kathy.Abshire@chkd.org and Kimberly.Day@chkd.org are the probable addresses, unverified. Administrative side line: 757-668-7084, listed as CHKD's authorized-official number in federal NPI records, purpose unverified. Mailing address: 601 Children's Lane, Norfolk, VA 23507.
Principal
Amy Sampson, President and CEO since December 2022, for a principal-to-principal approach once finance has engaged.
Facilities seat
The VP of Facilities and Support Services role, which carries Real Estate, is in transition. Jason Mueller held it from April 2025 and has left (one directory shows him as CEO of Sentinel Healthcare Solutions in 2026). No successor was posted as of this report. The seat reported to Dr. Christopher Foley, Senior Vice President and Chief Clinical Operations Officer, per the hiring announcement, so Foley owns Facilities and Real Estate until it is refilled and is a legitimate third name for the letter. Older directories still list Tamika Harris; she is also gone.
Basis
$10.03M paid in 2016. Add roughly ten years of carry (real estate tax on C-2 land at City Center plus opportunity cost) and CHKD's make-whole number is in the $11M to $13M range.
Sale
Comparable C-2 and C-4 land at Oyster Point and City Center trades roughly $500K to $800K per acre, so $7.5M to $12M on 14.93 acres. The deal clears near basis, not below it. Expect an ask at $12M or more.
Ground lease
At a 6 to 7 percent land yield on an $11M value, $660K to $770K per year, with CPI bumps. Keeps the asset on CHKD's balance sheet and avoids a nonprofit asset-disposition process.
Carve
VNG needs 10 to 12 acres. Offering to leave CHKD a 3 to 5 acre pad on the Jefferson Ave side gives them a future medical office site and lowers your price.

Opening line that works: you know the parcel, the date and the price. A regulated utility HQ is a credit tenant and a quiet neighbor. Lead with the ground lease, then let them ask about a sale.

2 · City Center Expansion Area

Newport News
East of City Center at Oyster Point, between Canon Blvd and Rock Landing Dr · was #2
Proximity
Adjacent to City Center. About 1 mile to 746 Diligence Dr, about 1.5 miles to Port Warwick.
Acres
38, from which a 10 to 15 acre employment anchor would be carved.
Owner
City of Newport News / EDA.
Zoning
C-4 Oyster Point Business District; mixed-use, commercial and employment uses by right.
Status
One of 12 sites marketed at the city's EDGE Summit (Oct 2025); the city says it is seeking proposals.

The public alternative to the CHKD tract, with the same address quality. The difference is process: a city disposition runs through an RFP, master-plan conformance and council approval, so expect 9 to 18 months to a signed agreement against 60 to 120 days with a private owner. Use it as the credible second option that keeps CHKD's price honest, and as the fallback if CHKD will not deal.
