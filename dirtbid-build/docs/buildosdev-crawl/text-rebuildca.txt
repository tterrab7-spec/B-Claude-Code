THE REALITY

Rebuilding is overwhelming.

After a disaster, homeowners are forced to make massive financial and construction decisions under stress — often while navigating dozens of disconnected vendors, timelines, and opinions.

Insurance confusion

Claims, scope letters, and policy limits no one explains plainly.

Contractor uncertainty

Bids that don't match, vendors who vanish, quality you can't verify.

Permit delays

County queues, plan corrections, and zoning rules slowing every decision.

Cost overruns

Change orders and surprise allowances eroding your insurance proceeds.

Too many vendors

Architects, engineers, expediters, builders — all uncoordinated.

No clear roadmap

No single timeline showing what happens next, or who owns it.

BuildOS was created to simplify the process.
