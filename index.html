Before you enroll

Straight
answers

The questions the enrollment line gets every day.

How long do the programs take?
Both the Class A Barber and Cosmetology tracks are 1,000 hours. How fast you bank those hours depends on the schedule you pick, and there are flexible options for students who already work. Payment plans run 6, 9 or 12 months.
Do I need experience to start?
No. You need to be 17 or older with a diploma, GED or equivalent, a valid ID, and the permit and registration fees. Everything else you learn here.
What does the $10,495 actually cover?
$8,995 tuition, the $250 registration fee, and $1,250 for your student kit, books and supplies. The $25 TDLR student permit fee goes to the state separately. Paying in full settles it at $9,500.
Can I work while I am in school?
Yes, and plenty of students do. That is what the flexible scheduling is for. Tell us your availability when you tour and we will show you which schedule fits.
How do I apply for the Yolanda DeLeon Scholarship?
Ask about it on your tour or when you call (512) 375-8142. A limited number are awarded each term and eligibility is reviewed before you sign a payment plan, so bring it up early.
Will I actually cut real people?
Yes. That is the whole point of the model. The school shares a building with a shop that is open to the public seven days a week, so you build speed and consultation skills on paying clients under a licensed instructor instead of on mannequins.
What happens after I graduate?
You sit for the state board with preparation built into the curriculum, and graduates get first look at open chairs in the shops this school is attached to. You also leave knowing how to price, book and run a chair as a business.
¿Enseñan en español?
Sí. La escuela y las barberías operan en inglés y español. Llame al (512) 375-8142.
