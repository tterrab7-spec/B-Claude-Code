Before you come in

Straight
answers

The questions the front desk gets every day.

Do I need an appointment?
No. Walk-ins are always welcome at both shops and we keep chairs open for them. That said, if you want a specific barber at a specific time, book it — Saturdays fill up.
How do I book with a specific barber?
Through theCut app. Search Country Cutz, pick your shop, pick your barber. If you would rather talk to a person, call the Bastrop shop at (512) 409-3211.
Can I get cut before 9 AM or after close?
Yes, by arrangement with your barber. Off-hours appointments are a flat $60 for a cut. Use the clock on the price list to see how that changes what you pay.
Do you cut kids?
All day. Kids 12 and under are $35 and our barbers are used to the wiggling. First haircut? Tell us when you sit down.
Is the Academy a separate business from the shop?
Different function, same building and same people. DeLeon Barber & Beauty Academy operates at the Menchaca Road location, which is why students train on real clients from day one instead of practicing on mannequins in a classroom.
How long do the programs take?
Both the Class A Barber and Cosmetology programs are 1,000 hours. How fast you finish depends on the schedule you choose, and there are flexible options for students who are working. Payment plans run 6, 9 or 12 months.
Can a haircut client be worked on by a student?
Only if you choose it. Student services are supervised by licensed instructors. Ask at the desk which chair you are being seated in.
¿Hablan español?
Sí. Both shops and the Academy operate in English and Spanish.
